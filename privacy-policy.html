Privacy Policy

App: Neringarent Assistant
Operator: Neringarent (holiday apartments in Juodkrantė, Lithuania)
Effective date: 25 November 2026

1. About this app

Neringarent Assistant is a private tool used only by the owner of the Neringarent TikTok account. It helps publish and manage content about holiday apartments in Juodkrantė and about the places, moods and life of the Curonian Spit. The app is not offered to the public.

2. What data we access

When the account owner logs in with TikTok, the app may access, with the owner's permission:

  • basic profile information (display name, avatar, username);
  • profile statistics (follower count, likes, video count);
  • the list of the owner's videos and their public statistics;
  • the ability to upload and publish videos to the owner's account.

The app does not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

3. How we use the data

Data is used only to publish content and to review the performance of the owner's own account. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing.

4. Service providers

The connection to TikTok is handled through Composio (composio.dev), an integration service that securely stores access tokens on behalf of the owner.

5. Data storage and retention

Access tokens are stored only as long as the connection is active. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or by disconnecting the app in Composio. After disconnection, the stored tokens are deleted.

Under the GDPR you have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data. Contact us using the details below.
